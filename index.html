Frequently Asked Questions

Is Waps free to use?

Yes! All core features — direct messaging, context menu, clipboard detection, country selector, history, and basic templates — are completely free, forever. PRO features are optional for users who need advanced productivity tools.

Does Waps automate sending messages?

No. Waps is a manual productivity tool. Every message requires the user to click "Send" in WhatsApp Web. It does not send bulk or automated messages. It simply opens the correct WhatsApp conversation for you — faster.

Does Waps collect or share my data?

Absolutely not. Waps stores all data (templates, history, preferences) exclusively in your browser's local storage using chrome.storage.local. No data is ever sent to external servers. We have zero analytics on your messages or contacts.

Is Waps affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta?

No. Waps is an independent productivity utility. It uses WhatsApp's official, publicly documented wa.me click-to-chat URLs to open conversations. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.

Can I get banned from WhatsApp for using Waps?

Waps does not violate WhatsApp's Terms of Service. It simply opens standard wa.me links — the same mechanism used by millions of businesses on their websites. It does not inject code into WhatsApp, automate actions, or scrape data.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a full 14-day money-back guarantee on all PRO subscriptions, no questions asked. See our complete Refund Policy below.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Privacy Philosophy: 100% Local (Client-Side)

Waps does NOT collect, store on external servers, transmit, or share any personal information, phone numbers, conversations, or message content. All processing occurs strictly locally within your web browser using standard Google Chrome APIs.

2. Information the Extension Processes

  • Phone numbers & recent history: If you enable "Recent History," numbers you enter are stored locally using chrome.storage.local. They never leave your device. You can clear all history at any time.
  • Quick message templates: Any saved templates are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage.
  • User preferences: Settings (theme, default country, feature toggles) are saved locally.

3. Chrome Permission Justifications

  • storage: Persist preferences, templates, and recent numbers locally.
  • clipboardRead: Optional auto-paste feature. No clipboard data is sent to any server.
  • contextMenus: Adds the right-click "Send WhatsApp to..." option.
  • tabs / webNavigation / scripting: Detects existing WhatsApp Web tabs to avoid opening duplicates.
  • Host Permissions (wa.me, web.whatsapp.com): Required to redirect to WhatsApp's official interface.

4. Third-Party Services

Waps opens conversations via WhatsApp's official click-to-chat URLs (wa.me and web.whatsapp.com). When you open a chat, WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy applies.

Any future changes will be reflected in this document with an updated date.
